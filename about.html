About KARU

KARU is designed to coordinate the ride market, not control it.

The product idea behind KARU is simple: riders make offers, drivers choose freely, and the platform provides structure without taking away marketplace decision power.

Core idea

A marketplace-first model with cleaner product logic.

Coordination instead of control

KARU does not impose a rigid fare menu or a forced driver flow. It organizes requests and responses so the market can function more directly.

Offers instead of fixed fares

Riders begin with an offer, and drivers retain the ability to accept, counter, or decline based on real context.

Driver choice stays visible

The driver experience is not treated as an afterthought. Choice is part of the product structure itself.

Simple direct presentation

This site keeps the story focused: product pages, legal pages, and support in one static deployable structure.

What this static site covers

  • Product positioning for riders and drivers
  • A clean landing page for the KARU model
  • Privacy, terms, and support pages ready to publish

Everything stays deployable as a plain static product site

No JavaScript. No backend. Just a complete multi-page website ready for publishing.
