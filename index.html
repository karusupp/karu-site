How it works

A ride flow that stays simple from offer to arrival.

KARU keeps the experience direct. Riders send offers, nearby drivers respond freely, and the trip is completed without the platform forcing the market.

01

Rider sends an offer

The rider sets the trip request and proposes a price instead of choosing from a fixed fare menu.

02

Nearby drivers accept or counter

Drivers review the offer and decide freely whether to accept it, reject it, or return a counter-offer.

03

Ride is completed directly

Once both sides agree, the trip moves forward with a direct and uncomplicated experience.
