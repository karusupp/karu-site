Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

KARU ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights when using the KARU platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Phone number, email address (for drivers), and name.
  • Location Data: Real-time location data to enable ride matching, navigation, and trip functionality.
  • Trip Data: Pickup location, dropoff location, trip duration, distance, and offer/counter-offer activity.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, app version, and basic diagnostics for performance and security.

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate the KARU platform (match riders and drivers)
  • Process trip requests and updates
  • Send verification codes (OTP) and important notifications
  • Improve performance and user experience
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud or abuse

3. Payments

  • Payments are handled by third-party payment providers (such as Stripe).
  • KARU does not store full credit card details.
  • Payment may be authorized before a trip and captured after completion.

4. Sharing of Information

  • We only share necessary data between riders and drivers during an active trip.
  • We may share necessary data with third-party services such as payment providers and notification systems.
  • We do not sell personal data.

5. Data Retention

  • We retain information only as long as necessary to provide the service.
  • We retain information only as long as necessary to comply with legal obligations.
  • We retain information only as long as necessary to resolve disputes.

6. Your Rights

  • Request access to your data
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data

Contact us to make a request.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

8. Children

KARU is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of KARU means you accept the updated policy.
